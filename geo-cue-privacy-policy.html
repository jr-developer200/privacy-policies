Part A: Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the GeoCue Android application ("GeoCue," the "app," or the "Service") accesses, uses, stores, and shares information. GeoCue is developed and published by Manoj Pedvi ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy applies to GeoCue and related support communications; third-party services have their own privacy policies.

1. Information GeoCue accesses and how it is used

Location and reminder information

GeoCue accesses precise or approximate device location to let you select your current location, create geofenced reminder areas, and detect when you enter or leave a saved place. If you enable a reminder, GeoCue collects location data for this purpose even when the app is closed or not in use. Reliable monitoring mode may use a visible location foreground service while active reminders exist.

Reminder text, saved coordinates, place names, radius, recurrence settings, reminder state, and local activity history are stored in the app's private storage on your device. GeoCue does not send this reminder content or your precise saved locations to us, Firebase, RevenueCat, AdMob, or other advertising partners. Android and Google Play services may process device location to provide the location and geofencing APIs used by the app.

App usage, diagnostics, and configuration

Release versions of GeoCue use Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics may process app interactions, app and device information, acquisition information, IP-derived approximate location, and identifiers such as a Firebase installation ID or advertising ID for analytics and attribution.
  • Firebase Crashlytics may process crash reports, stack traces, diagnostic logs, device model, operating-system and app versions, app state at the time of a crash, installation identifiers, and timestamps so we can diagnose stability problems.
  • Firebase Remote Config processes an installation identifier and related technical information to retrieve the minimum supported app version and other configuration values.

We use this information to understand general app usage, maintain compatibility, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and improve GeoCue. Crashlytics collection is disabled in debug builds.

Purchases

GeoCue uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to offer and restore the GeoCue Pro entitlement. Google Play handles payment details. GeoCue and RevenueCat may process an anonymous app-user or installation identifier, product and entitlement status, purchase tokens or receipts, order or transaction identifiers, timestamps, app and device information, IP address, and last-seen information. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card number.

Maps and place search

GeoCue downloads map tiles from OpenFreeMap. A tile request necessarily communicates technical request information and the map area represented by the requested tiles. OpenFreeMap states that it does not store IP addresses in regular server logs, although it may temporarily log IP addresses during a security incident. Place search uses Android's system geocoder; search text and related request data may be processed by the geocoding provider configured on your device under that provider's terms.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any diagnostic information or attachments you include. We use it to respond, provide support, protect the app, and meet legal obligations.

This information may be used to deliver ads, select contextual or interest-based ads where permitted, cap how often an ad appears, measure and report ad performance, attribute conversions, maintain and improve advertising services, and detect fraud and abuse. Depending on your region and choices, AdMob may serve personalized ads, non-personalized ads, limited ads, or ads needed only for technical delivery. GeoCue does not provide AdMob or its advertising partners with permission-based precise or background location, saved reminder coordinates or place names, reminder text, reminder history, exported backups, or purchase details for ad targeting. Google may independently infer a general location from an IP address and may combine information received from GeoCue with information from other apps, sites, or Google services in accordance with Google's policies and your settings.

Where required, GeoCue uses Google's User Messaging Platform or another Google-certified consent mechanism to request and record advertising and local-storage choices before requesting ads. If the in-app Privacy options control is available, you can use it to review or withdraw applicable consent. You can also manage or reset the Android advertising ID, delete the advertising ID where supported, and manage ad personalization in your device or Google Account settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal, and ads may continue in a non-personalized or limited form when legally permitted.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy, and Google's ad privacy controls. AdMob may make inventory available to additional advertising technology providers; Google maintains information about those providers through its advertising and consent interfaces.

3. Android permissions

Depending on your device and the features you enable, GeoCue may request or use:

  • Approximate and precise location to select places and detect reminder boundaries.
  • Background location to detect arrivals and departures and deliver active reminders when GeoCue is closed or not in use.
  • Notifications to display triggered reminders and, in Reliable mode, the ongoing location-service notification.
  • Location foreground service for optional Reliable monitoring mode.
  • Exact alarms only if you enable precise delivery for snoozed reminders.
  • Run at startup to restore active reminders after a device restart or app update.
  • Internet and network state for maps, place search, configuration, analytics, crash reporting, ads, purchase verification, and update checks.
  • Advertising ID, where available and permitted, for ad delivery, measurement, attribution, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.

You can decline or revoke optional permissions in Android Settings. Location reminders may not work correctly without the location, background-location, and notification permissions required for that feature. GeoCue does not request access to contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone, camera, installed-app lists, or broad file storage.

4. Local storage, exports, cookies, and deletion

GeoCue stores reminders, saved places, templates, activity history, and preferences locally until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can use Settings > Delete all local data to remove GeoCue's locally stored reminder data. GeoCue disables Android cloud backup for its private app data.

If you export a JSON backup, you choose its destination through Android's system document picker. The exported file can contain reminder text, saved coordinates, place names, and settings. That copy remains until you delete it and is subject to the privacy and security practices of the selected storage provider.

GeoCue does not directly use browser cookies for its core reminder features. AdMob and other service SDKs may use mobile identifiers, SDK storage, tokens, cached data, or similar technologies to remember privacy choices, deliver and measure services and ads, and prevent fraud. Web pages opened from the app may use cookies under the relevant website's policy.
